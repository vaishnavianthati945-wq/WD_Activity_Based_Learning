Development Activity-Based Learning** assignment. The work covers

bakery and cake shop.

## Case Studies

### 1. Tourism and Heritage Information Website

**Problem Statement**

Create a tourism website featuring historical monuments, cultural
festivals, famous destinations, local cuisine, travel tips, and image
galleries. Organize content by region and attraction type.

**Main content areas** - Historical monuments - Cultural festivals -
Famous destinations - Local cuisine - Travel tips - Image galleries

### 2. College Fest Management Website

**Problem Statement**

Build a college festival website displaying technical events, cultural
competitions, workshops, schedules, event coordinators, rules, and
registration forms. Use category-based cards and responsive event
banners.

**Main content areas** - Technical events - Cultural competitions -
Workshops - Event schedules - Event coordinators - Rules and
registration forms - Category-based event cards and responsive banners

### 3. Bakery and Cake Shop Website

**Problem Statement**

Create a website for a bakery displaying cakes, pastries, cookies,
custom cake designs, product prices, seasonal offers, and store contact
details. Use a visually appealing color scheme and image galleries.

**Main content areas** - Cakes, pastries, and cookies - Custom cake
designs - Product prices - Seasonal offers - Store contact details -
Image galleries
