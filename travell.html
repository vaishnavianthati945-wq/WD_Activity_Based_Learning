Cultural Festivals

Diwali

The festival of lights celebrated with lamps, decorations, sweets and family gatherings.

Holi

The festival of colors celebrating joy, friendship and the arrival of spring.

Onam

Kerala's harvest festival featuring flower decorations, traditional feasts and boat races.

Famous Indian Cuisine

Hyderabadi Biryani

Aromatic rice prepared with spices and traditional cooking techniques.

South Indian Dosa

A crispy rice-and-lentil crepe served with chutney and sambar.

Rajasthani Dal Baati

A traditional regional dish served with dal and baked wheat balls.

Travel Tips

  • Research the destination and local weather before travelling.
  • Respect local customs, traditions and religious places.
  • Carry identification, essential medicines and drinking water.
  • Keep historical monuments and natural surroundings clean.
  • Check entry fees, opening hours and transport options in advance.
